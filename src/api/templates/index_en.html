What is Prima

Verifiable proof that your creation already existed.

Prima lets you register prior-existence evidence of ideas, documents and digital files through the Bitcoin network.

Each registration generates a unique cryptographic fingerprint that makes it possible to check the existence and integrity of the content at a given date.

Proof of prior existence

Prove that your content existed before a verifiable date.

Digital integrity

Check that a document or file has not been modified since it was registered.

Independent verification

Validate the evidence with public tools, without depending on Prima.

What it is for

  • Register an idea or project before presenting it.
  • Document scripts, designs, photographs and digital works.
  • Certify versions and deliveries to clients.
  • Generate evidence of developments, code and documents.
  • Back the prior existence of a creation in case of disputes.

Compatible with documents, images, audio, video, compressed files and text.

MCP connection

You can also use prima from your AI assistant through MCP, the open standard that lets assistants use external tools. Connect it once and ask your assistant: “certify this file”. It registers your project files, keeps the copy of the original in a folder on your computer and tells you how each registration is going.

  • Your files stay on your computer: only their fingerprint is registered.
  • One folder per project, for your originals and your certificates.
  • Works with Claude, Codex and other MCP-compatible assistants.
